Kith is a free personal and portfolio project that helps you keep track of the people and companies in your network. By creating an account or using Kith, you agree to the terms below. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

Kith is provided free of charge, as-is, with no guarantees. It is a side project, not a commercial product or a professional service. Treat it accordingly and keep your own backup of anything important (use the in-app "Export my data" button).

What we collect

  • The email address and password you use to create an account. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text.
  • The information you choose to add: people, companies, roles, notes, tasks, and contact details you type or paste in.
  • Basic technical logs needed to run and debug the service.

How your data is used and stored

  • Your data is private to your account. Other users cannot see it.
  • Data is stored on third-party cloud infrastructure (such as the hosting provider and managed database). Text you paste for extraction may be processed by a third-party AI model provider to pull out people and companies.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Security is handled on a best-effort basis. No online service can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so please do not store passwords, financial details, government identifiers, or other highly sensitive information in Kith.

Your choices

  • You can export all of your data at any time from the in-app "Export my data" button.
  • You can delete your people, companies, and tasks within the app. To have your whole account removed, contact the maintainer.
